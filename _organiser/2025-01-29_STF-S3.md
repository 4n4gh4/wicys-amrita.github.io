## Report

The WiCyS Amrita Student Chapter hosted an engaging and informative session titled **Dictionary Attack Using Linux** with speaker Vedhavarshini Vijayakumar, a 2nd-year B.Tech CSE-CYS student, and the voice of our club. The event aimed to introduce students to dictionary attacks, a common password-cracking technique, and demonstrate the practical application of ethical hacking tools such as *rsmangler* and *JohnTheRipper.*

The session began with an introduction to dictionary attacks, explaining their significance in cybersecurity. The key topics covered included:
- **Understanding Dictionary Attacks** – A theoretical overview of how attackers use precompiled wordlists to crack passwords.
- **Introduction to rsmangler** – Demonstrating how to generate customized wordlists for targeted password attacks.
- **JohnTheRipper in Action** – Hands-on demonstration of how JohnTheRipper can be used to test password strength and improve security measures.
- **Ethical Hacking & Cybersecurity** – Discussing how ethical hackers leverage these techniques to strengthen digital security.

The session attracted students from various disciplines, ranging from beginners to cybersecurity enthusiasts. The interactive nature of the session allowed participants to ask questions and try out tools in real-time, enhancing their hands-on experience.

- Password security is crucial in defending against cyber threats.
- Dictionary attacks exploit weak passwords, emphasizing the need for strong and unpredictable credentials.
- Ethical hacking plays a vital role in improving cybersecurity practices.
- Tools like *rsmangler* and *JohnTheRipper* can be effectively used for both offensive and defensive security research.

The event successfully provided valuable insights into dictionary attacks and their impact on cybersecurity. Participants left with a better understanding of password security and the importance of ethical hacking. The WiCyS Amrita Student Chapter continues to foster knowledge-sharing and hands-on learning experiences in cybersecurity, encouraging students to explore further in this ever-evolving field.
